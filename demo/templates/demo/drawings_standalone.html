Checkbox, toggle and switch

A host project with neither django-mvp nor Cotton: daisyUI's stylesheet and nothing else. The same page inside the django-mvp shell

Drawn in Python

Three boolean fields, each drawn a different way. The first states nothing and is a checkbox, the second is a toggle by its name in the form's statement, and the third is a switch in the layout. Turn some on, submit the form, and the page shows what the form cleaned to.
