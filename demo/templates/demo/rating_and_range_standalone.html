Rating and range

A host project with neither django-mvp nor Cotton: daisyUI's stylesheet and nothing else. The same page inside the django-mvp shell

Drawn in Python

Two fields that each hold one choice, drawn as a rating, one star for each choice, and a number field drawn as a range. The first rating states its drawing by its name in the form's statement and cannot be cleared. The second states it in the layout and has an empty choice, which clears it. The range states it in the layout, and its limits and step are the ones the field declares. Pick a star, move the slider, submit the form, and the page shows what the form cleaned to.
