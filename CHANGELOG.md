## [Unreleased]

### Added

- A field that holds one choice, a `Select` or a `RadioSelect` such as a `ChoiceField`, a `TypedChoiceField` or a `ModelChoiceField`, can be drawn as daisyUI's rating, with one star for each choice. State it as the checkbox, toggle and switch are stated, with `Choice(drawing="rating")` in a layout or by the field's name in `FormChoices(fields=...)`. The stars are bare radio inputs with the field's name, in the field's order, and a person picking one submits the value of its choice, so the form validates and cleans exactly as it does with a select or a radio group, and the form's own widget is not changed. A choice whose value is the empty string is the way to clear the rating and is drawn first as the input daisyUI hides, and a choice whose value is `0` is a star. The stars are one group, in a fieldset with a legend, a required marker, help text and errors, as a radio group is, and each star is named by its choice's label. A field in error draws its stars in the error colour, `bg-error`, and a class or an attribute you set on the widget is on every input. A form that states no rating is drawn as before.
- A number field, an `IntegerField`, a `FloatField` or a `DecimalField` with Django's `NumberInput` or a subclass of it, can be drawn as daisyUI's range, a slider. State it with `Choice(drawing="range")` in a layout or by the field's name in `FormChoices(fields=...)`. It is one `<input type="range">` with the field's name and id, drawn through a copy of the widget, so the form's own widget is not changed. Its `min`, `max` and `step` are the ones Django writes on the widget from the field, the pack adds none, and a bound or initial value is the slider's value. The form validates and cleans exactly as it does with a number input, and the label, help text, errors, required marker, disabled state and the classes and attributes you set on the widget are kept. A field in error carries `range-error`. A slider always submits a number, so an optional number field drawn as a range is never submitted empty, and an extra form of a formset that holds a range is always submitted as changed. A form that states no range is drawn as before.
- A rating and a range take the size and the colour of the form and of the field, like any input, and have no variant: a variant stated for the form is passed over for them and one stated on the field raises `InvalidChoice`. A range takes `range-xs` to `range-xl` and `range-neutral` to `range-error` on its input. A rating takes `rating-xs` to `rating-xl` on the group of stars and daisyUI's background colour classes, `bg-neutral` to `bg-error`, on each star and never on the input that clears it. A field in error is drawn without the chosen colour and keeps its size, with `bg-error` on a rating's stars and `range-error` on a range.
- A demo page, "Joined groups", at `/joined-groups/` in the django-mvp shell, and the same page at `/joined-groups/standalone/` on daisyUI's CDN build alone. They show a form to submit with a country code and a number under one label, a group that already fails in one member, a group with help text on a member, a group with a disabled, a read-only and a hidden member, a group with no label and a group of one, and a group at each size, at each colour and in the variant, a coloured group with one member in error and a group inside a `Choice`.
- A joined group, `Join` from `mvp_forms.layout`: a layout object that names several fields to be drawn as one daisyUI join under one label, such as a country code and a phone number. The inputs are the direct children of one element with `join`, in the layout's order, in one fieldset whose legend is the group's label with the required marker. Each input is named by its own field's label and keeps its own help text, errors, `aria-describedby` and `aria-invalid`, and the form posts and cleans to the same data as with the fields apart. A group holds field names, `Field` and `Choice`, whose attributes and choices reach the inputs, and its `css_id`, `css_class` and attributes are on the element with `join`. A hidden member is drawn beside the join, a disabled member and a read-only member stay as they are, a group with no label has no legend, and every form of a formset drawn stacked draws the group. A member is an input or a select: any other field, a rating or a range, and any other layout object in a group, raises the new `InvalidMember`, a `ValueError` carrying `member`. A floating label never applies to a member. Three templates are new and listed in "Replacing one template": `daisyui/layout/join.html`, `daisyui/layout/join_member.html` and `daisyui/field_messages.html`, which `daisyui/field_body.html` now includes for the help text and errors it drew itself. A form that holds no `Join` is drawn as before. Every member takes the form's size, colour and variant, a `Choice` around the group or one member wins over them, and a member in error carries its error modifier and no colour. The README's new section, "Joined groups", says what a group holds and what raises.
- A demo page, "Floating labels", at `/floating-labels/` in the django-mvp shell, and the same page at `/floating-labels/standalone/` on daisyUI's CDN build alone. They show a form to submit whose input, textarea and select float, whose nickname undoes the form's statement and whose checkbox is passed over, a form that already fails, a disabled and a read-only field, and a form with no layout that floats one field by name, and a floating form at each size, at each colour and in the variant.
- A floating label, daisyUI's own, stated in Python as `label="floating"` in `FormChoices` for a whole form or in `Choice` for one field, in a layout or by the field's name. It is drawn for an input, a textarea or a select, through `{{ form|crispy }}` and `{% crispy form %}` alike, and in a formset drawn stacked. The label is drawn once, inside the field and tied to the input, with the required marker, help text and errors as before, and the form cleans to the same data. An empty field with no placeholder of its own shows its label's text as one. The form's statement passes over a field that cannot take it and a disabled field keeps its ordinary label; the same statement on a field that cannot take it raises `InvalidChoice` with `kind="label"`. A floating field takes the form's size, colour and variant, and its own, as any field does. The README's new section, "Floating labels", says what takes one and what does not.
- A host project can replace any one template of the `daisyui` pack with a file at the same path, found by Django's own template loading, with no setting, no Python and no change to a form. The README's new section, "Replacing one template", lists every template the pack distributes with what it draws, the names it is handed and where a replacement is found: through `TEMPLATES` for the templates outside `daisyui/widgets/`, and through the form renderer for the ones under it, which the default renderer finds only in an app listed before `mvp_forms`. It gives a worked example, and says the development server has to be restarted after a replacement for one of the five templates django-crispy-forms keeps in memory. From this release the listed paths and the names each template is handed are part of the public API. When a listed template changes, the old path or name keeps working for one minor version: a replacement at an old path is still drawn and raises a `DeprecationWarning` naming the old path and what replaces it, and a project with nothing there sees no warning. The CHANGELOG of that release says what changed and what replaces it. The markup and the class names inside a template are not part of that promise. No template changes in this release.

### Changed

- `InvalidChoice` for a drawing now names the drawings the field takes in `allowed`: the three of a boolean field, `rating` for a field that holds one choice, `range` for a number field, and none for any other field. A rating stated for a multiple select, a checkbox group, a null-boolean select, a text input, a number field, a boolean field, or a select or radio group that names a template of its own raises it, naming the field, and so does a range stated for a field that is not a number field, which includes a localised one.

## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-03

### Added

- A demo page, "Multi-widget field", at `/multi-widget-field/` in the django-mvp shell. It shows `MultiWidgetField` on a required split date and time with a different placeholder on each part, and a split date and time with no layout object, in a form to submit, which comes back with an error in the field's frame when it is submitted empty, and in a form that already fails. The same forms are on the standalone page at `/decorated-fields/standalone/`, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `MultiWidgetField` from `crispy_forms.layout` is drawn by the `daisyui` pack, and so is a `SplitDateTimeField` with no layout object, which earlier releases left to a later one. Each part of a multi-widget is drawn as the daisyUI component of its kind: the two parts of a split date and time carry `input`, and `input-error` when the field fails, and a class of your own on a part is kept beside them. The size, colour and variant stated for the form or with `Choice` reach every part, and a failing field's parts carry the error modifier and not the colour. The frame is one `<fieldset>` with one `<legend>`, one help text and one error element, which describe it. Each part has an `aria-label`: Date and Time, translatable, for a split date and time, the field's label for the parts of any other multi-widget, and one you give a part is kept. A sequence of attribute sets goes on the parts in order, one set goes on every part, and fewer sets than parts leaves the rest without them. A part made hidden gets no class and no name. `cleaned_data` is unchanged. The classes and names are written on a copy of the widget for each render, so the form's own widget and its parts are not changed and drawing a form twice gives the same markup. The README says the parts of a multi-widget are drawn as inputs, where it said fields with any other widget carry no class.
- A demo page, "Inline field", at `/inline-field/` in the django-mvp shell. It shows `InlineField` on a required email and a required city, each with no label and named by its placeholder, and on a checkbox that keeps its label, in a form to submit, which comes back with an error in each of the first two frames when it is submitted empty, and in a form that already fails. The same forms are on the standalone page at `/decorated-fields/standalone/`, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `InlineField` from `crispy_forms.bootstrap` is drawn by the `daisyui` pack with no visible label: the frame holds no `<label>` element for the field, the input has an `aria-label` equal to the field's label, and a text input or a textarea has a `placeholder` equal to it unless the widget sets one. A placeholder or an `aria-label` of your own is kept, and a label marked safe reaches both as text. A select and a radio group get no placeholder and are named by `aria-label`, the group on its fieldset. A single checkbox keeps its `<label>` with the checkbox inside. Help text and errors are drawn as for any field and describe the input, which has `aria-invalid` and its error modifier when it fails. `css_class`, `wrapper_class` and `template=` work, and `cleaned_data` is unchanged. A field on the same helper that is not inline keeps its label.
- A demo page, "Uneditable field", at `/uneditable-field/` in the django-mvp shell. It shows `UneditableField` on an account with its value and the `disabled` attribute, beside an editable nickname, in a form to submit. The account is declared disabled in the form class, so submitting the form keeps its value and shows no error. The same form is on the standalone page at `/decorated-fields/standalone/`, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `UneditableField` from `crispy_forms.bootstrap` is drawn by the `daisyui` pack with the field's current value shown and the input disabled, with the `disabled` attribute and no class: daisyUI draws the look from the attribute. A select, a checkbox, every option of a radio or checkbox group and a textarea are disabled the same way. The label and help text are drawn and tied to the input, a value holding markup is escaped, and the form's own field and widget are not changed. The browser does not submit a disabled input, so a form that needs the value kept declares the field `disabled=True` in the form class. `uneditable-input` is not drawn on an input a person can see, and only that name is dropped, so a class of your own called `active` on an input is still drawn. The README no longer says the pack writes no `disabled` attribute: this is the one case where it does.
- A demo page, "Field with buttons", at `/field-with-buttons/` in the django-mvp shell. It shows `FieldWithButtons` with a field that has one button and a field that has three, in a form to submit, which comes back with an error in each frame when it is submitted empty, and in a form that already fails. The same forms are on the standalone page at `/decorated-fields/standalone/`, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `FieldWithButtons` from `crispy_forms.bootstrap` is drawn by the `daisyui` pack as daisyUI's `join`: one `join` element holding the input, which carries `join-item`, and then every button you gave it, in order. The buttons are drawn once, by the same code as a button anywhere else, so a `Submit` keeps its name and value and button content holding `{{ ... }}` from a value is escaped and never evaluated. The frame keeps its one label, help text and error element, and a failing input has its error modifier. `css_id`, `css_class` and extra attributes go to the `join` element, `template=` draws your own template, and `input_size` is accepted and does nothing. On a checkbox or a radio group the field and the buttons are both drawn, and a hidden field is drawn alone. The input and the buttons take the size stated for the form or with `Choice` around the layout object; a size stated for one field by name reaches the input but not its buttons. Attached text takes the same size on its wrapper, and the input inside carries none.
- A demo page, "Inline choices", at `/inline-choices/` in the django-mvp shell. It shows `InlineRadios` and `InlineCheckboxes` in a form to submit, which comes back with an error in each group when it is submitted empty, and in a form that already fails. The same forms are on the standalone page at `/decorated-fields/standalone/`, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `InlineRadios` and `InlineCheckboxes` from `crispy_forms.bootstrap` are drawn by the `daisyui` pack as a radio group or a checkbox group with the options along a line, wrapping onto the next line when there are many or their labels are long. Each option is still an input inside a label of its own, the frame is still a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, and checked, disabled and grouped options, errors and `cleaned_data` are those of the group drawn without them. A widget that names a template of its own is drawn by it, and a field that is not a group is drawn as it is without the layout object. Nothing changes for a group that is not wrapped in either.
- A demo page showing attached text: a form with a prepended text, an appended text, both, and a prepended text on a select, all required, that comes back with an error in each frame when it is submitted empty, and a second form that already fails. It is inside the django-mvp shell, and the same forms are on a new standalone page, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone, that the pages for the other layout objects that decorate a field are added to.
- `PrependedText`, `AppendedText` and `PrependedAppendedText` from `crispy_forms.bootstrap` are drawn by the `daisyui` pack as daisyUI's label inside an input: one `input` wrapper, which is a `<label>`, holding a `<span class="label">` for each text that is set and the input, which carries no `input` class of its own. On a select the wrapper is `select`, and the error modifier is on the wrapper. The text is markup, so anything a person typed has to be escaped before you pass it in. On a checkbox, a radio group, a date drawn as three selects, a textarea or a file input the field is drawn as it is without the layout object, and a hidden field is a hidden input alone. `input_size` and `active` are accepted and do nothing.
- `wrapper_class` on a layout object or on `Field` now reaches the frame's outer element, a `<div>` or a `<fieldset>`, as django-crispy-forms documents. It was ignored before.
- A boolean field can be drawn as a checkbox, a toggle or a switch, chosen in Python one field at a time. `Choice` gains a fourth keyword argument, `drawing`, stated in a layout, as `Choice("publish", drawing="switch")`, or by the field's name, as `FormChoices(fields={"notify": Choice(drawing="toggle")})`. A toggle and a switch are daisyUI's toggle: the same checkbox input with the class `toggle`, so the form posts and cleans as it does with checkboxes and the pack adds no script. A switch also carries `role="switch"`, so assistive technology announces it as a switch. A field that states no drawing is drawn exactly as before, and `checkbox` is the ordinary drawing. Only a boolean field takes a drawing: a name that is not one of the three raises `InvalidChoice` with `kind="drawing"` and the three names allowed, and any drawing stated for a field that is not a boolean field, or around a button, raises it with nothing allowed. A toggle and a switch take the form's size and colour and the field's own, like any input, as `toggle-sm` or `toggle-primary` where a checkbox takes `checkbox-sm` or `checkbox-primary`. They have no variant: one stated for the form is passed over and one stated on the field raises, as for a checkbox.
- A demo page, "Checkbox, toggle and switch", at `/drawings/` in the django-mvp shell and at `/drawings/standalone/` styled by daisyUI's CDN build alone. It draws one form with three boolean fields as a checkbox, a toggle and a switch, which posts and shows what it cleaned to.
- A demo page, "Size, colour and variant", at `/choices/` in the django-mvp shell and at `/choices/standalone/` styled by daisyUI's CDN build alone. It draws a small form for each size and each colour, every kind of input at one size, the input variant, a button bar with a button in each variant, and a form that overrides what it states for one field in its layout, one field by name, one field's colour and one button. The forms are built from the tables of `Modifiers`. The README lists every allowed name for inputs and for buttons.
- Mistakes in a size, colour or variant are reported where the form is drawn, with the filter and with the tag. `InvalidChoice` carries `kind`, `value` and the names `allowed`, and `target` names the field, or the button, when the choice was stated on one: by name in `FormChoices(fields=...)`, with a `Choice` in a layout, or around a button. A mistake in what the form states for its buttons is reported by `{{ form|crispy }}` too, which draws no button. A variant stated for the form is passed over for a checkbox, a radio group and a checkbox group, and raises when stated on one of them; any choice stated on a field whose widget the pack does not cover raises. A `helper.daisyui` that is not a `FormChoices` raises `TypeError`, a page variable named `daisyui` that is not one is ignored, and a name in `FormChoices(fields=...)` that is not a field of the form raises `UnknownField`.
- Buttons take the form's choices. A `Submit`, `Reset`, `Button` or `StrictButton`, in a layout or added with `helper.add_input`, takes the form's `size` as `btn-sm` and the like, and a colour and a variant stated apart from the inputs' as `FormChoices(button_color="neutral", button_variant="outline")`, so `color` and `variant` reach no button. `Choice(Submit("delete", "Delete"), color="error")` gives one button a choice of its own. A `Submit` given a colour is drawn without the `btn-primary` it carries by default, and a `btn-primary` you pass as `css_class` is kept. Your classes, id and attributes are kept, a `Hidden` is not changed, and a form that states nothing draws its buttons exactly as before.
- Size, colour and variant can be stated once for a form, and every input the pack draws takes daisyUI's modifier for each. Set `helper.daisyui = FormChoices(size="sm", color="primary", variant="ghost")` on the form's helper instance, with `FormChoices` from `mvp_forms.choices`, and it works with `{{ form|crispy }}` and `{% crispy form %}`. The names are daisyUI's own: sizes `xs` to `xl`, the eight semantic colours, and `ghost` for text-like inputs, textareas, selects and file inputs. A choice an input has no modifier for, such as `ghost` on a checkbox, is passed over for that input, and a name daisyUI does not have raises `InvalidChoice`. Every option of a group, every select of a date and the removal checkbox of a held file take the choices, a field in error keeps its error modifier and is drawn without the chosen colour, and a form that states nothing is drawn exactly as before.
- One field can state its own size, colour or variant. In a layout, `Choice("search", size="lg")` draws what it holds with that choice, and it holds a `Row`, a `Fieldset` or another `Choice`, the inner one winning for what it states. `helper["search"].wrap(Choice, size="lg")` wraps a field already in a layout. For a form without a layout, `FormChoices(fields={"search": Choice(size="lg")})` names the field. A `Choice` wins over the field's entry in `fields`, which wins over the form's, each of the three on its own, and `None` undoes the form's choice for that field.
- A demo page showing alerts: a form with three alerts ahead of its field, one you can dismiss, one without a dismiss control and one with a daisyUI colour, that comes back with all three drawn again when it is submitted empty. It is inside the django-mvp shell, and the same form is added to the standalone containers page, which now draws a tabs element, an accordion, a modal and an alert with daisyUI's CDN build alone.
- `Alert` from django-crispy-forms is drawn by the `daisyui` pack as a daisyUI `alert`: an element with `role="alert"` holding your content as markup, so anything a person typed has to be escaped before you put it there. It carries a dismiss button of type `button`, named by an `aria-label` and translatable, which removes the alert from the page and never submits the form; `dismiss=False` draws none. Add a daisyUI modifier such as `alert-warning` through `css_class`. `block=True` is accepted and `alert-block`, which daisyUI does not define, is not drawn; like `tab-pane` and `active` it is dropped from the `css_class` of buttons, a `MultiField`, a `Tab` and an `Alert`. With it `TabHolder`, `Tab`, `Accordion`, `AccordionGroup`, `Modal` and `Alert` are all supported.
- A demo page showing a modal: a form whose street and city, both required, sit in a modal that a button on the page opens by the modal's id, so submitting it empty comes back with the modal open and the errors inside it. It is inside the django-mvp shell, and the same form is added to the standalone containers page.
- `Modal` from django-crispy-forms is drawn by the `daisyui` pack as a daisyUI `modal`: a `<dialog>` with the id you give, holding a `modal-box` with the title, the fields and one close button of type `button`, so closing never submits the form and the fields are submitted with the rest. The pack draws no control that opens it: your page opens it by its id, for example with a button that calls `showModal()`. It is drawn open when a field inside it has an error, also with the helper's `form_show_errors` off and inside a tab or an accordion group in the modal, and never open for an error outside it. The title is an `<h3>` named by the dialog's `aria-labelledby`, and the close button's text is translatable.
- A demo page showing an accordion: a form to post whose third group holds a required field, so submitting it empty opens the third group, and a form whose first group the developer closed and second the developer opened. It is inside the django-mvp shell, and the same forms are added to the standalone containers page.
- `Accordion` and `AccordionGroup` from django-crispy-forms are drawn by the `daisyui` pack as daisyUI `collapse` elements, each group a `<details>` with a `<summary>` holding the group's name and its fields, with no script. A group is open when django-crispy-forms marks it active: the first group when no field is in error, a later one you gave `active=True`, and the first with a field in error, also a hidden field, including inside a `Tab`. Groups open and close on their own, and the accordion draws no input or button of its own.
- A demo page showing tabs: a form to post whose second and third tabs hold required fields, so submitting it empty opens the second, and a bound form that already fails and opens the third. It is inside the django-mvp shell, and the same forms are on a standalone page, styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone, that later pages for the accordion, the modal and the alert are added to.
- `TabHolder` and `Tab` from django-crispy-forms are drawn by the `daisyui` pack as daisyUI `tabs`, each tab a radio named by the tab's name followed by its content, with no script. Exactly one tab is open: the first that holds a field with an error, or the first, also when you gave the first `Tab` an `active` argument. A tab's radio is not submitted with the form, and each holder has its own group name, so several holders and nested holders on one page do not open each other's tabs. Class names written for other template packs, now including `tab-pane` and `active`, are not drawn, so a class of your own named `active` on a button, a `MultiField` or a `Tab` is no longer drawn.
- The demo project has four more pages that draw a formset stacked and as a table, each with a version styled by daisyUI's CDN build alone. Each draws a formset to submit and a formset that already fails with a field error, a form-wide error and a formset-wide error, and posting the first with bad lines comes back with all three.
- A formset's delete and order inputs are drawn as the pack's checkbox and number input in both layouts, and in the table each has a column, a heading and an `aria-label` of its own. A form with no delete field, such as an extra form when `can_delete_extra` is off, leaves its cell empty so every row lines up with the headings, and a form marked for deletion keeps its delete input ticked when it is drawn again after a failed post.
- A formset's errors are drawn where they belong in both layouts. Errors for the formset as a whole, such as a minimum or maximum number of forms, were drawn nowhere and are now drawn once in an alert ahead of the forms, with `formset_error_title` when the helper sets it, and `{{ formset|as_crispy_errors }}` draws them on their own. In the table layout a row's form-wide and hidden-field errors are drawn in its first cell, and the row's `aria-describedby` names them. With `form_show_errors` off, none is drawn.
- A formset can be drawn as a table by setting the helper's `template` to `daisyui/table_inline_formset.html`. It is one daisyUI `table` with a row per form and a heading per visible field, each input named by an `aria-label` and described by its help text, and each form's hidden fields in the first cell of its row. A helper's layout is not applied in the table, and a formset with no forms draws no table.
- A formset is drawn when you hand it to the pack, with `{% crispy formset %}` or `{{ formset|crispy }}`, and until now it raised `TemplateDoesNotExist`. It is drawn stacked: the management form once, then every form one after another in a container of its own, each drawn as a single form is, with its hidden fields inside it. A helper's `form_tag` gives one `<form>` around the whole formset, its layout is applied to each form, and its media and buttons are drawn once.
- Disabled and read-only fields are drawn from the attributes already on the input, and the pack adds no class or attribute of its own for either. A field with `disabled=True` has `disabled` on its input, on every option of a radio or checkbox group and on a file field's removal checkbox, with its component class and its value (a password input has none). A `readonly` attribute on a widget reaches the input unchanged. Read-only is the browser's and applies only to text inputs and textareas.
- A hidden field's errors are drawn in the form-wide error element, as Django words them, `(Hidden field name) message`, escaped. Before, an invalid hidden field was drawn with its error nowhere, so a form failed with nothing on the page to say why. The element is drawn through `|crispy`, `{% crispy %}` and `|as_crispy_errors`, even when the form has no other form-wide error, and not at all with errors off.
- A file field is drawn as daisyUI's `file-input`, filling the width of its field, and an invalid one carries `file-input-error` and is described by its error. A `ClearableFileInput` whose field already holds a file shows a link to it; an optional field also offers a removal checkbox in a label of its own, tied to it by `for`, and a required one offers none and is not marked `required` in the browser, so the form can be submitted without choosing another file. A widget that allows several files keeps `multiple`, and a file name is escaped. A widget subclass that names its own template is drawn by that template.
- A `RadioSelect` is drawn as a group of daisyUI `radio` inputs and a `CheckboxSelectMultiple` as a group of `checkbox` inputs, each in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` that is described by the field's help text and error. Every option is an input inside a `<label>` of its own, tied to it by `for`; the held options are checked, an invalid group's options carry `radio-error` or `checkbox-error`, choices with named groups sit under their name, and an attribute your widget sets on one option stays on that option. Options of a required checkbox group are not marked `required`. A widget subclass that names its own template or its own option template is drawn by it.
- A boolean field is drawn as daisyUI's `checkbox`, inside its own `<label>` that is tied to it by `for` and carries the required marker. The box is ticked when the field's value is true, an invalid one carries `checkbox-error` and is described by its error, help text describes it, and with labels off it is named by an `aria-label` and no label is drawn. A checkbox keeps its natural size and is not widened.
- A date drawn as three selects by `SelectDateWidget` is one field in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>`, one help text and one error element, and each select is named Year, Month or Day by an `aria-label`. A field with several inputs that share one label is framed this way, and a field with one input keeps its `div` and `label`. The pack's own templates need a form renderer that loads Django templates, which the default renderer and `TemplatesSetting` both do. A widget subclass that names its own template is drawn by that template.
- The pack carries a base English catalogue for the text it adds.
- A field with choices is drawn as daisyUI's `select`, filling the width of its field: a `ChoiceField`, a `MultipleChoiceField`, a `NullBooleanField` and choices with named groups, which become `<optgroup>`s. The held choice is selected, an invalid select carries `select-error` and is described by its error, and a class or `data-` attribute you gave the widget is kept.
- The `daisyui` template pack for django-crispy-forms. Select it with `CRISPY_ALLOWED_TEMPLATE_PACKS = ["daisyui"]` and `CRISPY_TEMPLATE_PACK = "daisyui"`, and a form's text, email, URL, number, password, date, time and date-time inputs and textareas are drawn as daisyUI components with no layout written, each filling the width of its field. A field with any other widget is still drawn in place.
- Each field is drawn with its label, a required marker, its help text and every error message. The label is tied to the input, and the input is marked required, marked invalid and described by its help text and errors, so assistive technology announces them with the field. Labels, help text and errors are escaped.
- Errors that belong to a form as a whole are drawn once, in an element with `role="alert"`, through `|crispy`, `{% crispy %}` and `|as_crispy_errors`.
- Through `{% crispy %}` the pack draws the form element and its CSRF token, and honours the `FormHelper` settings for the form element, labels, errors, `label_class` and `field_class`. `help_text_inline` and `error_text_inline` are ignored.
- A demo page showing every text input in every state, inside the django-mvp shell and as a standalone page styled by daisyUI's CDN install alone.
- `Fieldset`, `Div`, `Row` and `Column` from django-crispy-forms are drawn by the `daisyui` pack: a daisyUI `fieldset` with its legend, a `div`, and a row that sets its columns side by side on a wide page. Your `css_id`, `css_class` and attributes are kept, and a container given `template=` is drawn with it. A demo page shows them, inside the django-mvp shell and as a standalone page.
- `Submit`, `Reset`, `Button`, `StrictButton`, `ButtonHolder` and `FormActions` from django-crispy-forms are drawn by the `daisyui` pack as daisyUI buttons in one container that wraps on a narrow page, and buttons added to a form helper with `add_input` are drawn after the fields inside the form element, as the same element a layout draws. A `disabled` attribute is kept, and class names written for other template packs, such as `btn-inverse`, are not drawn. The demo's layout objects pages show them.
- `HTML` and `Hidden` from django-crispy-forms work in a layout drawn by the `daisyui` pack. An `HTML` object is drawn where it is placed, including inside a `Fieldset`, `Column`, `ButtonHolder` or `FormActions`, with markup in its context values escaped, and a `Hidden` is an `<input type="hidden">` with no class and no generated id. The demo's layout objects pages show both.
- `MultiField` from django-crispy-forms is drawn by the `daisyui` pack as a daisyUI `fieldset` whose `<legend>` is its label, with each field inside it drawn in its own frame with its own label, help text and error. Your `css_id`, `css_class`, `label_class` and attributes are kept, and class names written for other template packs, such as `ctrlHolder`, `blockLabel` and `error`, are not drawn. With this, all thirteen layout objects of django-crispy-forms that structure a form or add buttons to it are drawn, and the demo's layout objects pages show each of them.
- A second demo page showing every select, boolean, radio and checkbox group, file and hidden input in empty, held-value, required, help-text, error and disabled states, plus a text input drawn disabled and another read-only and a multipart form to submit, inside the django-mvp shell and as a standalone page. A file you submit is checked and dropped, never stored.

### Changed

- The frame drawn around each field now lives in `daisyui/frame.html`, and `daisyui/field.html` only asks the pack how the field is drawn and includes the frame. A host project that overrides `daisyui/field.html` to change the frame has to override `daisyui/frame.html` instead.
